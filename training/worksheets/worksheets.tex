\chapter{Student Worksheets}

Worksheets are working documents, not decorations. Each one exists to preserve a decision, expose uncertainty, or make a final product easier to review. Students may complete them on paper, in a spreadsheet, or in a project Markdown file, but the final project folder must contain a completed version or equivalent evidence.
