\chapter{Assessment Rubrics}

The rubrics are designed to reward research quality rather than speed. A student may produce a modest project and still meet a high standard if the work is reproducible, cautious, source-backed, and useful for continuation. Suggested weights can be adjusted for course credit, stipend programs, or noncredit research training.

\section{Performance Levels}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.18\textwidth}Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Meaning}\\
\midrule
Exemplary & Ready for dsDNA Core archive or external sharing after normal proofreading. Evidence is traceable and decisions are clear.\\
Proficient & Meets the program standard with minor revisions. The main work is reproducible and scientifically cautious.\\
Developing & Useful progress, but one or more quality gates need revision before the project can move forward.\\
Incomplete & Missing evidence, unclear methods, unsupported claims, or outputs that cannot be rerun.\\
